About

I examine how AI-mediated learning environments reshape educational opportunity, particularly for linguistically and socioeconomically diverse learners. My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, instructional design, and educational equity.

Current affiliation: Johns Hopkins University, School of Education.
