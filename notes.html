How I got here

origin

Learning first felt like a private shelter.

I grew up treating books, languages, and small observations as ways to make sense of the world before I had the vocabulary for it.

shift

Business taught me systems; education taught me stakes.

Moving between marketing, history, and education made me more attentive to how institutions shape what people are allowed to imagine.

now

I keep returning to questions of access.

Who gets support, who gets judged, who gets translated poorly by systems, and who has to work hardest to be understood.
